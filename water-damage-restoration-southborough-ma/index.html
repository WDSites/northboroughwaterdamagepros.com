A water problem in Southborough, MA goes to the Northborough dispatch line, which sits about five miles northwest and answers around the clock. When a crew is available it comes the same day, for ZIP codes 01772 and 01745. The town is small, with school campuses and older village centers.

The calendar drives most of its water damage, so this page is organized by season.

Winter and frozen pipes

State insurance guidance ties burst-pipe coverage to a house that was kept heated and was not left empty. In Southborough, where big roofs and long winters meet, that matters, and a house left cold for a week is the classic claim problem.

Open cabinet doors under sinks during cold snaps, keep the heat on and know where your main shutoff is. If a pipe bursts, close the main, open a low faucet and call the line. The crew will extract, dry the cavity and set equipment.

When water is spreading in Southborough

Call at once when water is running through a ceiling, when a basement has taken in water, when sewage is backing up or when a frozen pipe has split in a wall. Ice dams on a large roof can push water into attic insulation and down through ceilings, and that is also an emergency.

Before the crew arrives: act on these four things

1

Close the main valve

Shut off the water where the main enters the house. For a split pipe, drain the line by opening a low faucet.

2

Protect the electrical

Turn off breakers only from dry footing. If a ceiling is dripping near a light fixture, switch it off and stay clear.

3

Move and photograph

Lift belongings out of the water and photograph the damage before it is moved.

4

Call the line

Tell dispatch the source, the affected rooms and whether the house has been heated.

Spring thaw, reservoirs and basements

The Sudbury Reservoir covers about a tenth of Southborough, and the town has a lot of open space, roughly 35 percent of the land. Spring thaw and heavy rain can raise water in low areas, and basements near wet ground take in seepage.

Basement water is a flood damage restoration job when it comes from outside, and it is a water mitigation job when a supply line is the cause. If you cannot tell which, call and describe where the water first appeared.

Summer storms and the villages

Southborough's villages, Cordaville, Fayville and Southville, each have older homes and small commercial buildings. Route 9 and Route 30 run east and west through town and Route 85 runs north and south, so most addresses can be reached by main roads. Summer thunderstorms bring roof leaks and gutter overflow, which calls for water damage repair after drying.

Westborough borders Southborough to the west and Marlborough to the north, and calls from either come to the same dispatcher.

Campuses, large roofs and big properties

Southborough is home to private school campuses, including Fay School and St. Mark's School, along with many larger houses with complex roofs. A large roof means more valleys, flashing and gutters, and an ice dam on a roof like that can put water into several rooms at once.

For a larger property, tell dispatch the building type, the number of floors and whether the heating system is running. A bigger scope needs more equipment and planning, and the sooner dispatch knows, the faster crews can prepare.

ZIP code coverage in Southborough

Southborough is covered under ZIP code 01772 for most of the town and ZIP code 01745 for the Fayville area. Dispatch handles every address in both ZIP codes. The Cordaville commuter rail station and the Route 9 corridor are both inside the covered area.
