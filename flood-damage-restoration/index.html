Flood damage restoration in Northborough, MA covers water that came from outside the plumbing system, such as storm runoff, rising groundwater or a backed-up sewer line. Because that water is not clean, the work is slower and more careful than a simple leak. Dispatch is open 24/7, and same-day response is available when crews are available.

If water is coming into the basement now, stay out of it. Floodwater can hide electrical hazards and carry bacteria. Turn off power to the area only from dry footing, keep children and pets away, and call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk before calling us.

When flooding is an emergency

Flooding is an emergency when water is rising, when it has reached outlets or appliances, when it smells like sewage or when it entered through the foundation during a storm. A basement that gets even a few inches of storm runoff can lose carpet, drywall and stored belongings in a day.

EPA guidance sets the drying window at 24 to 48 hours, because mold cannot grow without moisture. Floodwater also leaves silt and bacteria behind, which is why flooded materials are cleaned or removed, not just dried.

Basement flooding cleanup

Basement flooding is the most common flood call because basements are the lowest point of the house. The crew pumps out the water, removes carpet, pad and wet drywall to a safe height, then cleans and treats the framing and slab before drying begins.

Concrete and block hold more water than they look like they do. Drying a slab and a block wall takes longer than drying a wall of drywall, and the finishes should wait until moisture readings come down.

If flooding keeps coming back, restoration will not stop it. Grading, gutters, a sump pump or exterior drainage are the fixes, and a restoration company should tell you honestly that waterproofing is a different trade.

Sewage backup cleanup

A sewage backup is Category 3 water. It needs protective gear, removal of porous materials such as carpet and pad, and disinfection of what stays. Do not try to clean it with a mop and household cleaner, and do not run the washing machine or flush toilets while a backup is active.

The Town of Northborough's health department page directs sewer questions to the Department of Public Works and its Sewer Commission. Before you call a plumber or restorer, ask the DPW whether the blockage is in the town main or in your own lateral, because that decides who fixes it.

What not to do in a flooded basement

Do not walk into water if the panel or outlets could be submerged. Do not run a sump pump extension cord through standing water. Do not mix bleach with other cleaners. If anyone feels ill after contact with sewage water, call a physician.

Insurance and flood coverage

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance requires home insurance policies to carry a notice that flood damage is not covered, and says flood coverage is available through the National Flood Insurance Program or as an endorsement. Sewer backup is often a separate endorsement as well, so check your policy or ask your insurer.

Check the FEMA flood map for your address to see how your property is classified, and ask the Northborough building department about any permits before rebuilding.

What affects the cost of flood restoration

The key factors are the water category, the depth and the time it sat, the square footage affected, how much porous material has to be removed and how many days the structure needs to dry. Cleanup and disinfection add steps that a clean-water leak does not need.

After the water is out

Flooded homes usually move through three phases. First extraction and removal, then water damage cleanup to sanitize, then water damage repair to rebuild. Neighbors in Shrewsbury and Southborough follow the same sequence when a storm hits.

A tenant in a plaza such as Northborough Crossing or Post Road Marketplace should report a leak to the landlord the same hour, because the roof and drains belong to the building owner.

Flood Damage Questions

Do you handle flood damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Northborough?

Yes. Storms do not wait for business hours, and neither does dispatch. The line is open every hour, and crews respond the same day when available.

Does homeowners insurance cover a flooded basement?

Usually not. Flood sits outside standard Massachusetts home policies, a point insurers must now state in writing, so ask your agent about a separate flood policy and check how your policy treats sewer backup and sump failure.

Can flooded carpet be saved?

Carpet soaked by clean water can sometimes be dried. Carpet soaked by floodwater or sewage is usually discarded, because bacteria stay in the backing and pad.

Is floodwater cleanup the same as waterproofing?

No. Restoration repairs damage after water gets in. Stopping water from getting in is a drainage, grading or waterproofing job, and we will say so rather than sell you the wrong thing.
