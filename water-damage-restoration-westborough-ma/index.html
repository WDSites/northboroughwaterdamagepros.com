If water is still spreading: what to do before we arrive

1

Stop the source

Close the main water valve, or the valve at the fixture. If it is outdoor flooding, move to higher ground inside the house.

2

Cut power only if dry

Turn off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry and reachable. Otherwise leave it alone and call the utility.

3

Lift what you can

Get rugs, electronics, paper and small furniture up onto a dry surface or out of the room.

4

Keep people clear

Children and pets stay out of any water from a drain, a toilet or the outdoors. Then call the line.

Mill-era ground and what it means for your basement

Westborough's early industry ran along the Assabet River and Jackstraw Brook, where mills made boots, straw hats and other goods through the nineteenth century. The houses built around those mills are old, and many have fieldstone or early concrete basements that let in moisture through joints and floor slabs.

An old basement is not a reason to avoid finishing it, but it is a reason to know the wall is wet before you cover it. A moisture meter reading on the foundation, taken after a dry week, tells you what you are working with. If readings are high, flood damage restoration and drainage fixes come before finishing.
