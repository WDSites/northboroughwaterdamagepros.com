Water Damage in Northborough

Why the First Hours Decide the Outcome

Water damage rarely stays where it starts. A failed washing machine hose upstairs becomes a stained ceiling downstairs, and a storm that puts an inch of water in a basement can soak carpet pad, wall bases and stored belongings within hours. That is why our dispatch line is open 24/7 and why the first call matters more than the first repair.

The restoration industry follows a published standard, ANSI/IICRC S500, for water damage restoration, and S520 for mold remediation. It sets out water categories, drying goals and the use of moisture measurement. Any company, ours included, should be able to tell you whether its crews work to it.

Northborough homeowners have a few extra facts to keep in mind. Northborough's drinking water was supplied by the MWRA in 2022, according to the agency's community letter, and the town handles delivery from the meter outward. If water is coming from the street side of your meter, the town's water division is the call. If it is coming from inside your walls, close your own main and call us.

Roads, Buildings and Basements Around Town

Commercial buildings along Route 20 and Route 9 tie units together through common walls and roof drains, so one tenant's leak becomes the next tenant's emergency. In a center like Northborough Crossing or Post Road Marketplace, tell the owner at once, then call us with the unit number and the source. Route 135 and Main Street carry older houses where plaster, wood trim and stone foundations behave differently from newer construction.

Houses of all ages have basements, and basements are where most storm calls begin. Whether the water came from a failed sump pump, a split supply line or a backed-up drain decides the next step, which is why dispatch asks about the source first.

Secondary Service

When Mold Follows Water

Mold is a secondary service here, and it usually follows water. The EPA is blunt about the cause: mold cannot grow without water or moisture, and its answer is speed, with wet areas and items dried inside a day or two. Mold remediation finds the moisture, contains the area, removes contaminated material and dries the structure in line with S520 practice.

If a leak has been ignored, or a lender or buyer is asking about mold, read about mold remediation and call for an inspection. Health questions belong with a physician.

Questions About Water Damage in Northborough

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Northborough?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Crews respond the same day when available, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly if there is a wait.

What should I do before the crew arrives?

Stop the source if you can, usually by closing the main water valve. Turn off power to wet areas only from dry footing, lift valuables off the floor and photograph the damage. Call 911 or the utility first if water is near electrical equipment or you smell gas.

How is the price of a job set?

By the scope. After a walk-through and moisture readings, the work, the equipment and the pricing are explained before work starts. No exact price is possible from a phone description, which is why the real number follows an assessment.

Can you help with the insurance side of a water loss?

We provide photographs, moisture readings and a written scope of work that you can give to your insurer. The claim itself is between you and your insurer. Large losses and adjuster visits are easier when the drying log is complete.

Is water damage restoration the same as basement waterproofing?

No. Restoration removes water and repairs damage after it enters. Waterproofing and foundation work stop water from entering and belong to a different trade. We say so honestly when a recurring leak needs that kind of fix.

Is mold part of what you handle?

Yes, as a secondary service that usually follows water damage. Mold inspection, containment and removal are described on the mold remediation page.
