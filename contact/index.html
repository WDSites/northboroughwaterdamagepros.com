Call, do not wait

This site has no contact form on purpose. Water damage does not wait for a reply, so the phone is the only way to reach dispatch. Call (508) 446-9244 at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays. If water is near electrical equipment or you smell gas, call 911 or the utility first.

Have these ready

  • The address of the damage and the best phone number to reach you
  • Where the water came from, and whether it has been stopped
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and about how long the water has been there
  • Whether the water came from a drain, a toilet or outdoors
  • Photographs of the damage, if you can take them safely
  • The name of your insurer, if you plan to file a claim

What happens after you call

The dispatcher confirms the details and tells you what to do while a crew is arranged. Crews respond the same day when one is available, and you will be told honestly if there is a wait. On site, the crew assesses the damage and explains the scope and pricing before work starts.
